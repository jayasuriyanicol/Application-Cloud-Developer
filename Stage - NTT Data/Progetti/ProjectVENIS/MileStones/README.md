## Division MILESTONES

Every step from the scratch at the UX/IX reference was studied into steps milestones. In particular divided into:


## 1. First Milestone: *SCHCAL-1 Calendar CRUD & Shell* 

Creation of **DRAFT** summary system, in a nuthshell it include the steps of creation of the: **DETAIL SUMMARY** and **CONFIGURATION** with the **WEEKLY PATTERN** adaption.

## 2. Second Milestone: *SCHCAL-2 Rules & Generation* 

This was divided into two parts:

1. Creation of **RULES AND EXCEPTION**, in particular the defining system of a table with the order of the rules by the number.

2. Creation of **PREVIEW** from the **RULES AND EXCEPTION**, a system that create automatically by the period given in the Shell and other data. 
   All this was linked with the **ACTION POSSIBLE** to manage the possibility of what to do with calendar.


## 3. Third Milestone: *SCHCAL-3 Preview & Publish* 

The last part dedicated to fix the cohesion of the rules: e.g. format year of the School Calendar from *YYYY/YYYY to YYYY-YYYY* and extra definings as UX and IX.
